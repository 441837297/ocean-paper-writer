# Writing Unit Example

> Non-authoritative illustration. Current rules come from `SKILL.md` and the writing workflow.

## Confirmed Context

- section: Results
- logical result unit: magnitude and spatial structure of an EKE trend
- claim: EKE increased in the Kuroshio Extension during 1993-2022
- evidence: Fig. 3a-b
- boundary: mechanism attribution is handled by a later diagnostic unit
- target journal: JGR: Oceans

## JIT Reference Reading

The main agent invokes `references/subagents/reference-reading.md` for this question:

> How do recent JGR: Oceans Results sections open a quantitative spatial-trend unit and move from magnitude to spatial structure?

The subagent returns a compact brief with exact source headings, rhetorical sequence, evidence-to-claim progression, and wording that must not be copied. The source section itself is not added to the writing context.

## Draft Direction Confirmation

Before drafting, the assistant confirms in Chinese:

- 本单元先报告趋势量级，再报告季节与空间集中区。
- Fig. 3a-b 支撑直接结果；机制解释留在后续诊断单元。
- 现有材料缺少区域历史估计，暂标 `[CITATION NEEDED]`。

## Draft

```text
Eddy kinetic energy in the Kuroshio Extension increased by
12 +/- 4 cm2 s-2 per decade over 1993-2022 (Fig. 3a). The increase was
strongest in winter and concentrated between 145E and 160E (Fig. 3b).
```

## Author Check

- evidence used: Fig. 3a-b
- claim made: magnitude, period, season, and spatial domain of the trend
- boundary retained: this unit does not attribute the trend to a mechanism
- next choice: keep / revise / expand / continue
