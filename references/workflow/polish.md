# Polish Workflow

## Purpose

Polish is a focused Prose/Verify operation for confirmed text. It improves clarity, flow, precision, and natural academic expression without becoming a separate project stage or reopening the scientific strategy.

## Scope and Triage

The default unit is one paragraph; the maximum is one subsection. Check local dependencies, not the whole manuscript, unless a repeated number, core term, central claim, figure definition, method definition, or section responsibility changed.

| State | Action |
|---|---|
| Scientific meaning and unit function are sound | Polish locally |
| Evidence or citation gap | Mark `[EVIDENCE GAP]` and return to Strategy or the evidence owner |
| Architecture conflict | Return to Strategy and, after PASS, update the affected `03` owner |
| Terminology conflict | Confirm the term in `03_terminology.md` before revising |

## Rules

1. Preserve numbers, directions, evidence strength, terminology, citations, and uncertainty.
2. Remove vague subjects, unnecessary abstraction, generic transitions, inflated wording, repetitive rhythm, and scattered qualification.
3. Prefer a direct sentence with one main judgment.
4. Do not use vocabulary substitution as a substitute for clearer logic.
5. Do not create a new full manuscript version for an ordinary local polish.

Propose a revision direction, draft the candidate text, and send it to Verify when the edit materially affects meaning or a confirmed Revision Brief. Use `references/review/style-naturalization.md` plus at most one leaf module when needed. Abstract polish also loads `references/workflow/polish-abstract.md`.
