# Conclusions and Claims

## Purpose

Use this module when drafting or checking a Conclusion. It synthesizes confirmed findings,
implications, limitations, and useful next diagnostics.

Apply the global evidence boundaries in `SKILL.md` and the evidence-verb guidance in the writing
House Rules.

## Inputs

- confirmed primary finding and evidence source;
- Discussion interpretation and literature context;
- explicit limitation from the argument chain;
- current terminology.

## Structure

1. Restate the central finding.
2. Identify its evidence basis only when needed for clarity.
3. State the implication at the supported scale.
4. State the limitation that changes interpretation.
5. Name a specific future diagnostic when it follows naturally.

The Conclusion synthesizes evidence already established in Results and Discussion. Proposed broader
possibilities are labeled as future tests rather than current findings.

## Check

- Every scientific statement traces to the current manuscript.
- Finding, interpretation, and future test remain distinguishable.
- Scope and uncertainty match the argument chain.
- The closing sentence states a concrete implication or next diagnostic.
